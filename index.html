Pro Guidelines

Training & Grooming Master Tips

Crucial advice for tear-stain prevention, coat care, housebreaking, and preventing separation anxiety.

Training & Socialization

Overcoming Separation Anxiety

Bichons are affectionate "velcro dogs." From week 8, practice tiny absences (exit the room for 2 mins, then 5 mins) so he learns Lisa always returns safely.

Crate & Potty Routine

Take him outside every 30-45 minutes when awake and immediately after meals or naps. Reward with a high-value treat within 2 seconds of peeing outside!

Positive Reinforcement Cues

Bichons are sensitive to harsh tones. Use clicker training or enthusiastic verbal praise ("Yes!") paired with small soft treats.

Grooming & Tear-Stain Prevention

Daily Slicker Line-Brushing

Bichons don't shed onto furniture; trapped dead hair mats at the skin. Brush layer by layer down to the skin using a slicker brush and steel comb daily.

Preventing Tear-Stains

White Bichon fur easily shows tear discoloration. Feed filtered or stainless steel bowl water, high-grade food, and wipe eyes daily with saline pads.

Groomer Schedule (Every 4-6 Weeks)

Schedule professional fluff-dry trims, sanitary cuts, and nail clips every 4 to 6 weeks starting right after his 16-week final shots.
