Growth Journey

Puppy Development Timeline (Week 3 to 1 Year)

Track his milestones from a tiny 3-week puppy all the way to a full 1-year-old adult Bichon Frise powder-puff!

Week 3 • RIGHT NOW! With Breeder

Eyes & Ears Open, Shaky First Steps

His eyes and ear canals are now open! He is learning to walk on wobbly legs, wag his tiny tail, and play with his mother and littermates.

Weeks 4 – 5

Weaning, Soft Food & Social Play

Breeder introduces soft puppy gruel. First baby teeth emerge. Puppy plays actively, explores new sounds, and toddles around the puppy playpen.

Week 8 • HOMECOMING DAY! Pick Up Day

Bite Inhibition & Arriving at Lisa's House

Fully weaned with learned bite inhibition! Receives first DHPP core vaccine and microchip. Crate training and potty housebreaking start immediately!

Months 2 – 4 (8-16 Weeks)

Vaccinations, Cues & Socialization

Follow-up core shots at 12 & 16 weeks (plus Rabies). Prime window for positive reinforcement obedience (sit, stay, recall) and friendly social encounters.

Months 4 – 6

Teething & First Professional Groom

Adult teeth come in; provide soft rubber chew rings! Schedule his first official professional grooming haircut to familiarize him with blow dryers and scissors.

Months 6 – 9

Adolescence & Adult Coat Transition

Teenager phase! Soft puppy fur transitions to dense adult double coat; line-brushing every 1-2 days prevents mats.

Month 12 • 1 YEAR OLD! Full Adult

Mature Powder-Puff Companion

Reaches full adult weight (~12-18 lbs). Majestic white double coat fully matured. Loving, confident, cheerful lifelong companion for Lisa!

Pro Guidelines

Training & Grooming Master Tips

Crucial advice for tear-stain prevention, coat care, housebreaking, and preventing separation anxiety.

Training & Socialization

Overcoming Separation Anxiety

Bichons are affectionate "velcro dogs." From week 8, practice tiny absences (exit the room for 2 mins, then 5 mins) so he learns Lisa always returns safely.

Crate & Potty Routine

Take him outside every 30-45 minutes when awake and immediately after meals or naps. Reward with a high-value treat within 2 seconds of peeing outside!

Grooming & Tear-Stain Prevention

Daily Slicker Line-Brushing

Bichons don't shed onto furniture; trapped dead hair mats at the skin. Brush layer by layer down to the skin using a slicker brush and steel comb daily.

Preventing Tear-Stains

White Bichon fur easily shows tear discoloration. Feed filtered or stainless steel bowl water, high-grade food, and wipe eyes daily with saline wipes.
