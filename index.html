Care & Regimen

Essential guidelines for a healthy development.

The 5-Minute Exercise Rule

To protect growing joint plates, structured pavement walks should be limited to 5 minutes per month of age, up to twice daily. Free play in the house or yard is self-regulating and does not count toward this limit.

10 Mins / Walk

Tear-Stain Prevention

Bichon fur vividly shows tear stains. Utilize stainless steel or ceramic water bowls, provide filtered water, and gently wipe the eye area daily with a saline solution to maintain a pristine white coat.

Separation Independence

As a companion breed, they are prone to anxiety. Begin crate training early. Practice leaving the room for 1-5 minutes frequently so he learns independence and trust in your return.
