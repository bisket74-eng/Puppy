Care basics

Walk time

A common rule: about 5 minutes of walking per month of age, up to twice a day. Playing at home doesn't count.

Tear stains

White fur shows stains easily. Wipe around the eyes daily with a soft damp cloth and use clean, filtered water.

Alone time

Bichons love company. Start crate training early and practice short breaks, a few minutes at a time.

Grooming

That curly coat tangles. Brush a little every day and plan on regular professional trims.

Vet visits

Ask the breeder or vet about a shot schedule and when he can safely meet other dogs.
